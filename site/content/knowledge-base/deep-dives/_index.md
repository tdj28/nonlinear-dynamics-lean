Deep Dives are the durable textbook layer of the project. Each chapter starts
from first principles, gives the reader a physical and geometric mental model,
derives the mathematics without hidden steps, and then explains how that
mathematics is represented and proved in Lean.

Reading paths and prerequisites make the climb explicit. A newcomer can take
the full route; a specialist can jump to the theorem, implementation choice,
or proof boundary they need without losing context.
